## Quick Answer
Ajwain (carom seed) water is a traditional Indian drink for new mothers that may help with digestion, gas, and bloating after childbirth. Its main active compound, thymol, has shown digestive effects in lab and animal studies, though human studies in new mothers are limited.

## Body

### What Is Ajwain Water?
Ajwain water is a warm drink made by boiling carom seeds (Trachyspermum ammi) in water. It is a common postpartum remedy in Indian households, usually given to new mothers during the first 40 days after delivery.

### How Ajwain Water Helps After Delivery
The most documented benefit of ajwain is for digestion. Carom seeds contain thymol, an aromatic compound that:

- Stimulates gastric juice secretion, which helps break down food
- Has carminative effects, meaning it helps move gas through the digestive tract
- Shows anti-inflammatory properties in animal studies

A 2003 study published in Nutrition and Health analyzed traditional Indian lactation foods and found ajwain had the highest protein, fiber, and mineral content among all tested foods, including gond panjiri, kangni, and halwa.

### What the Evidence Does and Does Not Support
**Supported by research:**
- Ajwain can speed up digestion and relieve gas and bloating (animal studies)
- Thymol has shown effects against harmful bacteria like E. coli in lab tests
- Ajwain has anti-inflammatory effects in animal models

**Not supported by good evidence:**
- Ajwain water does not increase breast milk supply
- There is no evidence it helps with weight loss or belly fat
- Claims about "cleansing the uterus" are traditional, not research-backed

### How to Make Ajwain Water
1. Lightly dry-roast 1 teaspoon of ajwain seeds in a pan until fragrant
2. Add 2 cups of water and bring to a boil
3. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the water turns pale gold
4. Strain and sip warm

Some families add fennel seeds, cumin seeds, or a small piece of jaggery.

### How Much to Drink
One to two cups a day is a common amount. More is not better "” large amounts of strong ajwain water can cause heartburn.

### Safety While Breastfeeding
Ajwain is widely used in Indian cooking and the amount in ajwain water is small. It is generally considered safe in normal food amounts. If your baby seems unsettled after you drink it, stop and see whether it makes a difference.

### When to See a Doctor
Severe or worsening tummy pain, inability to pass gas or stool, fever, or pain around a c-section wound need medical attention. Ajwain water is for everyday discomfort, not for these symptoms.
